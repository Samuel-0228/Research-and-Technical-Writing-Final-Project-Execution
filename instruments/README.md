## Construct Operationalization & Scale Adaptations

All multi-item scales use a standard 5-point Likert response format:
- `1` = Strongly Disagree
- `2` = Disagree
- `3` = Neutral / Undecided
- `4` = Agree
- `5` = Strongly Agree

### 1. Source Trust Dimensions (Mayer, Davis, & Schoorman, 1995)
Evaluated separately across three source groups:
- **Senior Students (Near-Peers)**
- **Close Friends & Classmates**
- **Anonymous / Pseudonymous Posters**

For each source, items capture:
- **Ability / Competence:** E.g., *"Seniors understand the course requirements and academic policies well enough to give accurate advice."*
- **Benevolence:** E.g., *"When friends give advice on Telegram, they genuinely care about helping me succeed."*
- **Integrity / Objectivity:** E.g., *"Anonymous posters on Telegram provide honest and unbiased academic evaluations."*

### 2. Information Adoption & Perceived Quality (Sussman & Siegal, 2003)
- **Argument Quality / Usefulness:** Relevance, timeliness, accuracy, and comprehensiveness of the academic advice received online.
- **Source Credibility:** General confidence in the reputation and credibility of advice shared in Telegram student communities.

### 3. Reliance on Crowdsourced Advice
- Frequency of seeking academic advice in Telegram groups.
- Degree to which decisions are based directly on peer advice without independent administrative verification.
- Decisional influence: Extent of change in academic planning due to Telegram discussions.

### 4. Academic Decision Areas Affected (RQ2)
Multiple-choice and rating indicators tracking:
- Course registration / course load decisions.
- Instructor / section preferences.
- Department stream selection (Freshman placement).
- Selection of study guides, past exams, and reference materials.

### 5. Adverse Outcomes & Misguidance Experiences (RQ2)
- Frequency of encountering false, biased, or harmful advice.
- Specific consequences experienced (e.g., dropped a course unnecessarily, selected an unsuitable instructor, missed registration deadlines, unrealistic grade expectations).
- Brief open-ended incident capture for qualitative elaboration.

## Survey Administration Protocol

1. **Platform:** Self-administered online survey hosted via Google Forms.
2. **Access Control:** Deployed across batch, section, and department Telegram groups at Addis Ababa University.
3. **Informed Consent:** First page contains explicit informed consent, voluntary participation statement, and assurance of strict anonymity (no student IDs, names, or IP addresses recorded).
4. **Completion Time:** Estimated at 7 to 10 minutes based on pilot testing.
