# Research Report Directory

This directory contains the final research report documentation, required submission statements, and ethics declarations.

---

## Required Deliverables & Specifications *(Scheduled for Phase 5: Dec 10-13)*

According to the course guidelines, the final report deliverables will comprise:

1. **`final_report_template.md`** *(Phase 5)*: Academic manuscript structured according to the 8 required sections:
 1. Title Page
 2. Abstract (150-200 words)
 3. Introduction (Background, Problem Statement, Research Questions, Objectives)
 4. Research Methodology (Design, Sampling, Instruments, Data Collection, Analysis Plan, Limitations)
 5. Results (Reliability, Trust Comparisons, Regression, Decision Impacts)
 6. Discussion (Interpretation, Theoretical Alignment, Practical Implications)
 7. Conclusion and Recommendations (Actionable takeaways for AAU faculty and administration)
 8. References (APA 7th Edition)
 - Appendices: Additional statistical tables & Ethics Statement.
2. **`group_contribution_statement.md`**: A formal 1-page statement detailing each member's specific contributions to the research project.
3. **`ai_usage_declaration.md`**: A mandatory disclosure statement explaining whether and how generative AI tools were used, which tasks were supported, and how outputs were verified.
4. **`ethics_statement.md`**: Participant protection declaration covering voluntary participation, anonymity, and institutional protocol adherence.

---

## Formatting Standards

- **Length:** Maximum 10-15 A4 pages (excluding references and appendices).
- **Font & Spacing:** Times New Roman, 12pt, double-spaced, 1-inch margins.
- **Citation Format:** APA 7th Edition.
