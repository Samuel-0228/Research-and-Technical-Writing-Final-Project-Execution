# References Directory

This directory contains the bibliographic records, citation database, and synthesized literature matrix for the research project, formatted strictly according to the **American Psychological Association (APA) 7th Edition** style manual.

---

## Directory Contents

- **`references.bib`**: BibTeX database containing complete metadata for all cited peer-reviewed journal articles, conference papers, and institutional reports.
- **`literature_matrix.md`**: Comparative matrix detailing each study's theoretical foundation, methodology, geographical context, and relevance to crowdsourced academic advice.
- **`README.md`**: Guide to referencing conventions and literature review synthesis.

---

## Key Literature Foundations

1. **Multidimensional Trust:**
 - Mayer, R. C., Davis, J. H., & Schoorman, F. D. (1995). An integrative model of organizational trust. *Academy of Management Review*, 20(3), 709-734.
2. **Information Adoption & Online Heuristics:**
 - Sussman, S. W., & Siegal, W. S. (2003). Informational influence in organizations: An integrated approach to knowledge adoption. *Information Systems Research*, 14(1), 47-65.

3. **Peer Advice & Social Media in Higher Education:**

 - Cai, Y., Kamarudin, S., & Nujaimi, S. (2025). Willingness to share information on social media: A systematic literature review (2020-2024). *Frontiers in Psychology*, 16, Article 1567506.
