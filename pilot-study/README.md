# Pilot Study Documentation

This directory documents the pilot testing phase of the research instrument, designed to evaluate item clarity, completion duration, construct face validity, and preliminary scale reliability prior to full sample deployment.

---

## Objectives of the Pilot Study

1. **Clarity and Readability:** Identify ambiguous, double-barreled, or culture-specific phrasing within survey items.
2. **Administration Feasibility:** Measure time required for participants to read the consent form and complete all sections.
3. **Construct Sensitivity:** Verify that participants clearly differentiate between the three source types (*Seniors*, *Friends*, *Anonymous Posters*).
4. **Platform Usability:** Test mobile responsiveness on Telegram's in-app browser across Android and iOS devices.

---

## Pilot Sample Profile

- **Sample Size:** $N = 10$ undergraduate students from Addis Ababa University.
- **Composition:**
 - 3 First-Year (Freshman) students
 - 3 Second-Year Information Systems students
 - 2 Third-Year Computer Science students
 - 2 Fourth-Year Technology students
- **Format:** Combined cognitive walk-through / debriefing (5 respondents observed synchronously; 5 completed independently followed by asynchronous structured feedback).

---

## Summary of Pilot Findings & Refinements

1. **Completion Duration:**
 - Average completion time: 6 minutes 45 seconds (Range: 5.2 - 9.1 minutes).
 - Considered optimal for student survey retention.
2. **Key Adaptations Made:**
 - *Source Differentiation:* Clarified the definition of "Anonymous Posters" to explicitly include pseudonymous usernames and forward-only channels where author identity is hidden.
 - *Freshman Relevance:* Added a specific check in Section E for department placement choice so freshmen who have not yet chosen electives could still report high-relevance decisions.
 - *Scale Anchor Standardization:* Ensured consistent 5-point Likert labeling across all trust and quality subscales.

Detailed feedback logs and exact item adjustments will be documented in `pilot_feedback_log.md` and `pilot_protocol.md` upon completion of the pilot study in Phase 2 (Nov 26-29).
